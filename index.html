Monk

Monk is a private, single-user voice assistant that I built for my own use. It runs on my own computers and helps me with reminders, notes, drafting messages, and keeping an eye on my calendar and inbox.

What it does with Google

Monk connects to my own Google account with read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It uses that access to tell me about upcoming meetings and important unread email, and to answer questions such as "what's on my calendar tomorrow?". It cannot send email, change or delete messages, or edit calendar events.

Who it is for

Monk is not a public product and is not offered to other people. The only user is its developer.
